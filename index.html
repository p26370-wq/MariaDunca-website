ABOUT ME

I am a detail-oriented compliance and project management professional with eight years of progressive experience at Acquire BPO, where I advanced from Quality Assurance Specialist to Lead Compliance Officer. Throughout my career, I have gained extensive experience in planning and executing internal audits, coordinating non-technical projects, and managing document control systems. I work closely with cross-functional stakeholders to identify process gaps, implement corrective actions, and ensure that policies and procedures remain current and effective.

I am passionate about process improvement and compliance, particularly in ensuring that businesses operate efficiently while adhering to company standards, internal policies, and applicable government regulations. I believe that effective compliance goes beyond meeting regulatory requirements; it also involves identifying opportunities to improve processes, strengthen internal controls, and promote operational excellence.
